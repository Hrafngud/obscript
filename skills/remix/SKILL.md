# Remix

Create one unified knowledge model in PT-BR from independently analyzed sources.

- Detect semantic overlap even when sources use different terminology.
- Deduplicate claims and examples while retaining distinct evidence and meaningful nuance.
- Combine complementary arguments and choose the strongest or clearest examples.
- Surface contradictions. Reconcile them only when the supplied evidence permits it; otherwise preserve the disagreement and its provenance.
- Form a new defensible thesis and logical topic order based on the combined material.
- Keep topic provenance in `source_refs` or equivalent fields supplied by the schema.
- Preserve factual qualifications and do not manufacture bridges between unrelated claims.

The result must read as one knowledge model, never as sources placed back-to-back. Do not write the script or optimize wording for narration.
