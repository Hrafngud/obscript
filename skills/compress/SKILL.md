# Compress

Produce a smaller but logically complete PT-BR knowledge model for the requested duration.

Prioritize, in order: thesis, required argument chain, important facts, necessary context, examples, then tangents. Remove from the bottom first.

- Delete repetition and weak tangents.
- Merge examples that prove the same point and retain the clearest one.
- Condense background that is not required to understand later reasoning.
- Preserve caveats whose removal would make a claim misleading.
- Keep transitions implicit at this stage; the planner will design narration flow.
- Use the target duration and ordinary PT-BR speaking pace as a real content budget.

Return the transformed knowledge model only. Do not compress by rewriting every sentence more tersely, and do not write narration.
